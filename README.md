## 🔒 Privacy and Security Features You'll Love

### End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)

This isn't just a buzzword – it's real protection. Your files are encrypted on your device before they're uploaded to the server. Even if someone gains access to your cloud storage, they'll only see scrambled data. You hold the decryption keys, always.

### Secure Authentication

The app supports two-factor authentication (2FA) and OAuth2. This means even if someone steals your password, they can't get into your account without that second verification step.

### Local Data Control

Unlike many cloud services that force you to sync everything, you decide what stays local and what lives only in the cloud. Want to save disk space? Choose selective sync. Need files available offline? Pin them to your device.
